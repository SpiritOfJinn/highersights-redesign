Most insurance accepted

We're in network with many government and private plans. Not every provider takes every plan — the matching questionnaire only shows providers who take yours.

Good to know
  • Out-of-network? We can give you a superbill to submit for reimbursement.
  • EAP plans are not accepted.
  • We accept HSA cards and major credit cards.
  • Paying cash? You can request a Good Faith Estimate before care.
