Getting started

Three steps to your first appointment

Answer a few questions

Our matching questionnaire shows providers who take your insurance, fit your needs, and have openings — including their cash-pay rates.

We verify your insurance

Request at least 24 hours ahead so your provider can accept and we can confirm your coverage. Not seeing a good fit? Join the waitlist.

Meet your provider

Your first visit is an intake (up to 75 minutes) to talk about your history and goals and build a plan together. Regular sessions run about 50 minutes.

FAQ

Common questions

Why can't I book within the next 24–48 hours online?

Your provider needs time to accept the request and we need to verify your insurance. Once you're confirmed, you can message your provider in the portal to ask about an earlier time.

Does my provider take my insurance?

Most plans are accepted, but each one is verified individually. Check the billing NPI on your provider's profile and call your insurer to confirm they're in network — appointments may be canceled if a provider can't accept your plan.

The provider I want isn't showing up. Why?

They may not take your insurance, may not treat your specific concern, or may not have openings. Try another provider, choose telehealth to see more options, or join the waitlist.

I'm getting an "already a client" error.

The portal allows one email address per provider relationship. Use a different email to request a new provider, or call the front desk for help.

How much does a cash-pay session cost?

It depends on the provider and session length. The matching questionnaire shows each available provider's rate.

What should I expect at my first appointment?

Arrive 10 minutes early (or join your video link early). Your provider will ask about your background and what brought you in, and you'll set goals together. You can skip anything you're not ready to talk about.

How long are sessions?

Consultations are 10–15 minutes, standard sessions about 50 minutes, and first assessments up to 75 minutes.

What do I need for telehealth?

A device with a camera, a reliable internet connection, and a private space where you can talk freely.

How do I contact my therapist?

Current clients: log in to the client portal and use the Chat icon. The front desk answers calls and emails only during office hours — in an emergency, call 911 or 988.

How are providers screened?

Every provider is interviewed, passes federal and state background checks (re-run monthly), and has three professional references and their licensing-board history reviewed.
