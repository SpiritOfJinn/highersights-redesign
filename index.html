Getting started

Three steps to your first appointment

Answer a few questions

Our matching questionnaire shows providers who take your insurance, fit your needs, and have openings — including their cash-pay rates.

We verify your insurance

Request at least 24 hours ahead so your provider can accept and we can confirm your coverage. Not seeing a good fit? Join the waitlist.

Meet your provider

Your first visit is an intake (up to 75 minutes) to talk about your history and goals and build a plan together. Regular sessions run about 50 minutes.
